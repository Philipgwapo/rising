# Tubig — find water near you

Offline-first prepper app for the Philippines. Finds falls, springs, wells, lakes, reservoirs, dams and rivers within a radius of your GPS location or a town.

- **Install:** open https://philipgwapo.github.io/rising/ on your phone → menu → *Install app* / *Add to Home screen*.
- **Offline:** app + 10,908 built-in sources are cached after first open. *Save this area offline* stores live OpenStreetMap results for your area. Map tiles you have viewed are cached (up to ~3,000).
- **Directions:** open in Google Maps. Download your province in Google Maps → *Offline maps* for no-signal navigation.

Data: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Always treat water before drinking.
