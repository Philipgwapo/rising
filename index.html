      Make water safe before drinking
      • Clear water can still make you sick. Treat every source except a working tap.
      • Boil: bring to a rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes in high mountains).
      • No fire: filter through cloth, let it settle, then use chlorine drops or tablets as labeled and wait 30 minutes.
      • Take from moving water upstream of houses, farms and animals. Avoid green, oily or foul-smelling water.
      • Lagoons and river mouths can be salty. Salt water is not drinkable even after boiling.
      Where the data comes from

      Built-in: GeoNames gazetteer for the Philippines, which merges official names from the US NGA geographic names database with local edits. It covers named lakes, reservoirs, dams, falls, springs and about 10,000 rivers and creeks. River points usually mark the mouth, so walk upstream for cleaner water.

      Live (when online and allowed): OpenStreetMap, mapped by local volunteers, adds springs, wells, public taps and unnamed falls. Sources and areas you save stay on this phone only. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.

      Always confirm with the barangay before relying on a source. Some are inside protected watersheds or private land.
